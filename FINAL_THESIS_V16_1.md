# Humanity AI Circle V16.1 — Thesis Lock

V16.1 does not change the V16 thesis.

## Core
**People helping people through the AI transition.**

**Don't figure out AI alone.**

**People lead. AI helps.**

**Meet. Talk. Learn. Build. Belong.**

## What changed in V16.1
The website now expresses that thesis through a more visual, less repetitive story:

**YOU → PEOPLE → PROOF**

- **YOU:** What is changing for me? What do I need?
- **PEOPLE:** Who can I learn, meet, build or contribute with?
- **PROOF:** What does research say? What actually works? What stays human?

The founder/origin story is now explicit: the Circle is being built by enterprise AI practitioners, application builders and an applied AI research lab that repeatedly encountered the same gap between expanding AI capability and fragmented human clarity.
