# Humanity AI Circle — V2 to V14 Consolidation Record

V14 is a consolidation build, not a new thesis. It restores the strongest ideas that appeared across earlier versions while keeping the V13/V14 people-first transition model.

## What was preserved from the evolution

- **V2 / research foundation:** information is abundant, trustworthy evidence is scarce; outcome-first; context matters; human judgment matters.
- **V4:** real users, real tasks, real outcomes; people find each other; community lab.
- **V5:** fear is real, possibility is real; community as the bridge; trust is part of the product.
- **V6:** card-based visual storytelling; people and matching over long copy.
- **V7:** global signals, hype-to-evidence, evidence maturity, replication, human values.
- **V8:** concise multi-page information architecture.
- **V9/V10:** strongest emotional hook — “Don’t figure out AI alone”; pain recognition, benefits, before/after, real questions.
- **V11:** human community visual, social channels, question-first experience.
- **V12:** AI transition history, outcome-first / tool-second / brand-last, task articulation, human control, Weekly 30, roadmap.
- **V13:** deeper Explore / People / Themes / Research / Now / Roadmap channels.
- **V13.2:** restored conversion and evidence sections.
- **V14:** restores the ecosystem layer: curated paths through scattered AI information, I Need Help ↔ I Can Help, members-teach-members, contribution routes, trust/guidelines/privacy, and explicit current→future progression.

## Locked public thesis

**People helping people through the AI transition.**

**Don’t figure out AI alone.**

**People lead. AI helps.**

**Outcome first. Tool second. Brand last.**

**Bring your question. Bring your idea. Bring what you know.**

- **V15:** recurring human connection; global/local/topic/open connection model; AI can answer questions, people still need people.
- **V16:** UX/research/mobile certification layer; three-act homepage hierarchy, research editorial system, cross-page next steps, mobile-first flows, accessibility and deployment gates.
