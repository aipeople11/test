# Humanity AI Circle V16 — Certified UX Direction

## Frozen thesis

**People helping people through the AI transition.**

**Don’t figure out AI alone.**

**People lead. AI helps.**

**Outcome first. Tool second. Brand last.**

## V16 does not change the thesis

V16 turns V15 into a launch-oriented experience by improving hierarchy, research presentation, page-to-page flow, accessibility, and mobile responsiveness.

The homepage is organized around three visitor decisions:

1. **Recognize yourself** — why now, what feels unclear, what you need.
2. **Participate** — ask, connect, learn, meet, build and share.
3. **Trust the approach** — research, evidence, human control and transparent limits.

The Circle remains both digital and human: global conversations, recurring connections, topic circles and future local gatherings only where real member demand emerges.
