# Humanity AI Circle V16.1 — Visual Storytelling Pass

## Scope
No thesis rewrite. This pass changes homepage hierarchy, visual grammar, interaction and founder/origin storytelling while preserving the V16 strategy and deeper pages.

## Main changes
- Rebuilt the homepage around three true chapter canvases: **You / People / Proof**.
- Reduced homepage top-level content from 28 separate sections to 6 top-level blocks.
- Reduced visible homepage copy from roughly 2,213 words to roughly 1,027 words.
- Added an interactive **What is on your mind?** selector using progressive disclosure.
- Replaced repeated card grids with varied UI forms: timeline rail, question chips, convergence visual, two-sided help exchange, connection lines, recurring-connection orbit, outcome ribbon, research signal bars, evidence ladder, founder credibility block, human-control split and roadmap line.
- Added a sticky three-chapter story navigator with active-section state.
- Added subtle scroll reveal, ambient hero gradients, animated research bars and cross-page View Transition progressive enhancement.
- Added reduced-motion handling across motion effects.
- Strengthened the founder story using the user-supplied operating reality: enterprise AI use cases, working AI application building and an applied research lab.
- Updated the About page to explain why that practical work revealed the need for Humanity AI Circle.
- Preserved existing research/source links, community channels, privacy/guideline pages and subpage routing.

## Mobile
- Three-part story navigator remains visible and compact on mobile.
- Major visual flows transform vertically below tablet widths.
- Question selector becomes 2 columns on common phones and 1 column on very narrow screens.
- Connection orbit transforms into a non-overlapping grid.
- Research bars, evidence ladder, founder proof and human-control split all reflow without horizontal discovery.
- Existing V16 44px touch-target, focus and menu rules remain active.

## Content de-duplication
The homepage no longer repeats separate pain cards, real-question cards, multiple research summaries, multiple community loops and multiple evidence explanations. Each core idea appears once in the main story, with deeper detail routed to the relevant subpage.
