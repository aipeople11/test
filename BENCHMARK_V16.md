# V16 Independent Benchmark Framework

V16 should be evaluated as a potential-member decision journey, not merely as a visual website.

## Lenses

1. Strategy: problem → implication → action clarity.
2. Human: fear, curiosity, learning, isolation, work, trust and hope.
3. Community: ask, help, meet again, contribute, build and belong.
4. UI/UX: hierarchy, scanning, visual variety and cognitive load.
5. Journey: landing → recognition → trust → participation → return.
6. Research: source, context, population, methodology and interpretation.
7. Evidence: experience vs measured/verified/replicated evidence.
8. Trust: privacy, moderation expectations, sponsorship and human responsibility.
9. Technical: mobile, accessibility, links, SEO, performance and sharing.

## V16 launch questions

A first-time visitor should be able to answer quickly:

- Why does this community exist?
- Why is it relevant to me?
- What can I do here?
- How do people help each other?
- How do I keep connecting rather than attend once and disappear?
- How is research separated from hype?
- What counts as evidence?
- What remains human?
- What is real today versus a future possibility?
- Where do I go next?

## North-star reaction

> They understand what I am going through. I understand what this community does. I trust how they think. I can see how I would participate. I want to join.
